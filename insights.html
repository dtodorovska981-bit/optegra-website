Is your business franchisable? Four tests.

Building a franchise system can cost $20K to $100K+ before a single unit is sold. These are the four questions our readiness assessment answers first.

01 · Profitability

Does a unit make money?

After rent, labor and a royalty, a franchisee still needs a return worth the investment. If the margin only works without a royalty, the model needs work first.

02 · Repeatability

Can someone else run it?

Recipes, suppliers, hiring and daily routines have to be written down and teachable. If the business only works with the founder in it every day, it isn’t ready yet.

03 · Demand

Will it work in other markets?

Success in one neighborhood may depend on that neighborhood. We look at who the customers are and where else that customer lives.

04 · Scalability

Can the system support growth?

Supply chain, training and site requirements must hold at ten units, not just two. Growth breaks whatever was improvised.

For operatorsSite selection

What a site scorecard measures.

Rent is a ten-year decision, and visibility alone is not enough to justify a lease. We score every shortlisted site on the same factors so they can be compared side by side. The bars below show an example site, not a benchmark.

  1. Trade area

    Population, households and income within a realistic drive or walk time.

  2. Traffic & access

    Daily counts, turning movements, parking and how easy the site is to reach.

  3. Co-tenancy

    The neighbors that bring the right customers through the door.

  4. Competition

    Direct and indirect competitors, and whether the market is under- or over-served.

  5. Economics

    Rent, buildout and capex against projected sales at this specific site.
